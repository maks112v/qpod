# Changelog

- **V8.7**: Holding Centre on a Coverflow album opens the album's own menu: **Play next**, **Add to queue**, **Shuffle**, or **Go to artist** when the album has one. It follows the cover under the finger after switching **Sort**, and the **Sort** and **Refresh library** cards open nothing. Hold Play/Pause opens Now Playing; hold Centre opens a row's actions. Double Centre unlocks the lock screen. iPod: on Now Playing, short Centre cycles play modes and double Centre turns the screen off.
- **V8.6**: **System settings → Power management** gains **Charge limit**: at 80% the Q2 stops charging and runs from USB, and charges again at 75%, so a player left plugged in isn't kept full. It also gains **Low power**: with the screen off the second processor core sleeps and the player wakes 10 times a second instead of 20, and with the screen on but untouched the interface slows to 30 updates a second until you use it again; sound, EQ and brightness are unchanged. **Audio settings → Artists** can browse Artists by the **Album Artist** tag, so an album with guest artists stays one artist's; the artist page's own switch follows the same setting. Coverflow's new **Sort** card, before Refresh library, orders the covers by **Album**, **Artist** (then year), **Recently Added** or **Most Played**, and keeps your choice. Videos play their sound on a USB DAC too. Coverflow no longer shows an Unknown Album card when every song has an album tag.
- **V8.5**: Rockbox starts whenever the microSD card has it, as an iPod with Rockbox. Holding Play/Pause while powering on, or Rockbox's Boot stock OS, starts Q2 Pod for that session only, and the next power-on is Rockbox again.
- **V8.4**: Rockbox starts by default whenever it is on the microSD card, as on an iPod with Rockbox. Its Boot stock OS (or holding Play/Pause while powering on) switches to Q2 Pod, which then starts at every power-on until you hold Play/Pause again. A choice of Rockbox made on V8.3 carries over.
- **V8.3**: Rockbox can share the Q2 with Q2 Pod: unzip its `rockbox.zip` to the microSD card, then hold Play/Pause while powering on to switch between the two. The Q2 starts the system you chose at every power-on until you hold Play/Pause again. When Rockbox exits, or the card has none, Q2 Pod starts.
- **V8.2**: Most Played opens faster after its first open, reading the library again only after it changes. iPod: on Now Playing's lyrics page, the wheel scrolls the lyrics instead of changing the volume, and they follow the song again three seconds after the last turn.
- **V8.1**: Library puts what you play first: Shuffle Songs, then Artists, Albums, All Songs, Genres, Playlists and My Fav. Recently Added, Recent, Most Played, Frequent and Hi-Res follow, then Podcasts, Audiobooks, Photos, Books and Videos. Upload Scrobbles and Update Local Music move to the bottom, one wheel tick up from the top.
- **V8.0**: The equalizer draws its response above the list as you edit, with a ring on each band and the edited one filled, and its rows show the setting on the left with the value in grey on the right; the title names the preset you loaded. iPod: Now Playing gains a fourth page, a visualizer in the accent's colours: **Spectrum**, **Oscilloscope**, **VU Meters** or **Halo** (bars around the cover). Tap it to switch; your choice is kept. It shows the music as you hear it, EQ included. Now Playing's queue row and page are called **Queue** instead of Playlists.
- **V7.9**: Holding Play/Pause on an artist's albums opens the menu straight away, without first opening an album and going back. After that, holding on the artist's Songs tab opens the song menu.
- **V7.8**: The wheel takes a little more turn per tick, so a short list's middle row is easy to land on. Now Playing's album, Home's art and scrobbles follow playback into the next folder instead of keeping the last album. The Unknown Artist, Genre, Composer and Album rows only show when a song lacks that tag. Holding Play/Pause works on an artist's albums. **Most Played** lists your top 100. About's version row is now called **CFW. Version**.
- **V7.7**: New **Photos**, **Books** (.txt and .epub), **Videos**, **Podcasts** and **Audiobooks** rows in Local Music, each shown when its folder is on the microSD card. **Most Played** opens your top 25 with the artist and play count, and **Upload Scrobbles** sends your listens to Last.fm or ListenBrainz over Wi-Fi. Holding Play/Pause on a row adds Favourites, Add to playlist, Shuffle and Go to album or artist. Artists, albums and songs sort without a leading The, A or An, so The Cure is under C. A newly opened list scrolls at once, and the screen redraws fully when it turns back on. Key Tone stays quiet while music plays or headphones or Bluetooth are connected, so it only clicks on the speaker when nothing is playing or plugged in. iPod: Now Playing has bigger rounded art and a slim accent progress bar. About shows the stock firmware version again, with the Q2 Pod version on its own row; the main download is now the iPod build, and the stock-UI build is called Stock.
- **V7.6R / V7.6I**: Play counts: a song counts once you have heard half of it, or 4 minutes. **Most Played**, the second row of Local Music, plays your 25 most played songs, most played first. Scrobbling: the same songs are logged to `.scrobbler.log` at the root of the microSD card, ready for any Last.fm or ListenBrainz `.scrobbler.log` uploader. Coverflow lists an album's tracks in album order (disc and track number, else file name) and without file extensions. iPod: Home shows its arrow only on the highlighted row.
- **V7.5R / V7.5I**: The equalizer's **Preamp** row opens a list: **Auto** sets just enough cut for your boosts and follows band edits, or pick +12 to -24 dB to keep a level of your own. A band's gain is picked with the wheel from -24 to +24 dB, and picking one turns the band on; the ten default bands are now one octave wide (Q 1.41), so they work as a 10-band graphic EQ. Frequency and Q open their own menu: tap the value to type one on the on-screen number keys, or step it with **Raise** and **Lower**. The equalizer now works on music above 48 kHz. DSD always plays without the equalizer, so its data reaches the DAC untouched.
- **V7.4R / V7.4I**: Coverflow is wheel-only: touch no longer drags or taps the covers.
- **V7.3R / V7.3I**: iPod: on Now Playing, a single press of the centre button turns the screen off, as on every other page. iPod: a quick double press switches the wheel between volume and scrubbing through the track. iPod: away from Now Playing, the volume panel sits exactly over Quick Settings' brightness bar instead of half over it.
- **V7.2R / V7.2I**: The equalizer's **Balance** is one row that opens a list from L 12.0 dB through Centre to R 12.0 dB in 0.5 dB steps, starting on the current value, instead of two rows that stepped it 0.1 dB at a time. iPod: away from Now Playing, the volume panel shows a rounded bar and just the number.
- **V7.1R / V7.1I**: iPod: away from Now Playing, in Quick Settings or on a page without wheel navigation, the volume shows in a rounded panel near the bottom, with a white bar and "Volume 40", instead of stock's large slider.
- **V7.0R / V7.0I**: **Shuffle Songs**, the first row of Local Music, plays your whole library in shuffle from a random song. Coverflow opens on the album you last had in the middle, even after the player is powered off and on. Tracks of 20 minutes or more, such as mixes, audiobooks and podcasts, pick up where you left them when you play them again, even after a power-off; the last 32 are remembered. Seeking in a VBR MP3 now uses the file's own seek table, so it lands within about 1% of a long file instead of minutes off. iPod: turning the wheel on Now Playing shows the volume as a white bar in place of the progress bar, as on an iPod classic; the volume pop-up no longer dims the screen, on Now Playing or elsewhere.
- **V6.9R / V6.9I**: Album covers load as you scroll with the wheel, as they do when you scroll by touch; before, they waited until the next touch scroll.
- **V6.8R / V6.8I**: iPod: Display settings gains **Battery**: Icon, Percent, or Icon + Percent, which shows the level inside a horizontal battery that turns green while charging. A new Bluetooth codec badge such as AAC or LDAC shows for a second, then fades into the Bluetooth icon, which keeps the status bar tidy and leaves room for the percentage. In both releases a double press of the centre button has 200 ms instead of 180 to turn the screen off, so it is easier to hit; a single press opens a row 20 ms later. The parametric EQ takes up to 30 bands, and each band can apply to both channels or only the left or right. **Balance** corrects a left/right level mismatch in 0.1 dB steps by turning one side down. Equalizer APO presets with `Channel: L`, `Channel: R` and `Channel: all` sections import with each side's preamp, and blank OFF filter slots no longer stop an import.
- **V6.7R / V6.7I**: Coverflow opens quickly every time after its first open since boot, and reloads the album list only after Update Local Music, a folder scan or a deleted song. Browsing is faster too: stock wiped the player's file cache every 6 seconds, so the library and the card were read again and again, and it no longer does. Battery lasts an estimated 1-2% longer with the screen off, thanks to that fix, a player that wakes 20 times a second instead of 125 while the screen is off, and a crash check every 10 seconds instead of 2.
- **V6.6R / V6.6I**: iPod: a one-row turn of the wheel no longer overshoots. After a pause the first tick moves a row, and a second tick that slips through just after it is ignored; a fast spin and deliberate single ticks are not slowed. This replaces the **Wheel: Normal / Fine** setting, which is removed. The **Touch: On / Off** setting is removed too. The two Display rows that remain, **Accent** and **Home**, get icons like the rows above them.
- **V6.5R / V6.5I**: The clock keeps the right time after the player is powered off and on, in both releases; on stock it came back off by your time zone's offset, until the next Wi-Fi sync. Set the time once after updating, by hand or over Wi-Fi. With iPod's Graphite accent, lit switches, the − and + discs and OK buttons are a mid grey with their white knob, symbol or text clear on it, instead of white on bright silver.
- **V6.4R / V6.4I**: iPod: **Touch: On / Off** in System settings → Display. Off ignores the screen, so a stray tap or a finger resting on it does nothing while the wheel and buttons work as usual. The keyboard, quick settings, sliders and online pages need touch, so turn it back on from the same row with the wheel.
- **V6.3R / V6.3I**: Large VBR MP3s, such as hours-long mixes downloaded with yt-dlp, start playing at once in both releases; the stock player read the whole file off the card first, so they started late or seemed not to start. Seeking in a VBR MP3 is immediate too and lands by the file's average bitrate, so in a very long file whose bitrate varies a lot it can land seconds to minutes off.
- **V6.2R / V6.2I**: iPod: pages slide. Opening a list, a settings page or the equalizer slides it in from the right, pushing the page behind it out, and Return slides it back out, in 120 ms; the status bar stays still. Now Playing, Coverflow, pop-ups, quick settings and Tidal pages open at once, as before. In both releases the centre button opens a row 20 ms sooner: the double-press window for screen off is 180 ms instead of 200.
- **V6.1R / V6.1I**: iPod: **Wheel: Normal / Fine** in System settings → Display. Normal (the default) moves a row on every wheel tick as before; Fine takes two ticks a row, so single-row moves don't overshoot. Home always shows its selected row: Now Playing after a boot, otherwise the row last selected or tapped. In both releases, going back with Return after using touch shows the selection again. Long lists speed up more gently: two rows a step after 0.3 seconds of spinning, eight after 1.5. With Key Tone on, lists click once per row change, so not on a Fine half step or against a list end. The Normal release is unchanged apart from its version.
- **V6.0R / V6.0I**: iPod: with the Graphite accent the selection bar is a solid grey with a faint top edge and the status bar a plain darker grey, so the chrome sits back while the selected row stays clear under its white label. Other accents and the Normal release look the same.
- **V5.9R / V5.9I**: Faster in both releases. Coverflow draws each frame with about a sixth less work while the covers move, and the PEQ skips bands left at 0 dB, so a preset that uses only a few bands costs less battery during playback. Nothing looks or sounds different.
- **V5.8R / V5.8I**: Coverflow gets depth in both releases: the selected album faces you at its usual size, two neighbours a side turn inward behind it, dimmer the further out they are, and every cover has a faint reflection. Covers turn, move and fade together as you turn the wheel or drag, and the album and artist underneath change when the new album settles. Tap a side cover to bring it to the centre; tap the middle one to open its tracks. If the player is short of memory, Coverflow falls back to the flat covers. iPod: active quick-settings controls (Buttons lock, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the rest) now show in the accent's colour, clearly apart from the grey off ones, with a dark symbol on Graphite's silver so it stays readable. Settings icons keep their own colours under every accent, so Network Service, DLNA, Wi-Fi and Backlight are no longer turned pale.
- **V5.7R / V5.7I**: iPod: settings icons are drawn at their real 40-pixel size, resized smoothly when the firmware is built instead of scaled on the player, so their edges are no longer jagged. The status-bar icons sit 2 pixels further in from the rounded corners, and the title keeps one width between them, so a long title such as "System Setting" ends in an ellipsis even when few icons show. Scrubbing on Now Playing previews every wheel tick at once and jumps only when you press centre or Return, touch the screen or wait 3 seconds, so slow turns no longer stutter playback; leaving without turning doesn't seek. The normal release is unchanged apart from its version.
- **V5.6R / V5.6I**: iPod refinements across six screens. Settings lists show four complete 68-pixel rows with 40-pixel icons, clear of the rounded corners, so the last row's icon is no longer cut off. The status-bar title uses all the room the showing icons leave ("System Setting" in full) and Coverflow gets its title. Home has room above and below its seven rows, and in Full the chevrons stay clear of the corner. Quick settings labels line up on two even lines and brightness is a slim bar you can tap or drag. Now Playing's art and text keep 16-pixel margins, with separate space for the dots, bar and times. Coverflow's album name is larger than the grey artist, both clear of the corners. Confirm pop-ups have dark buttons with clear check and cross marks in every accent and a white frame on the selected one. The normal release is unchanged apart from its version.
- **V5.5R / V5.5I**: iPod fixes. On Home the wheel shows the selection bar again and every row has its chevron. The status bar icons are back: they sat under the screen's rounded corners, so they now keep clear of them, and EQ moves to the left next to the play state, as on stock. Home's labels, Now Playing's "3 of 12", its top icons, the progress bar and both times, and the text of the screen saver notes also stay clear of the corners, so none of them is cut off. The normal release is unchanged apart from its version.
- **V5.4R / V5.4I**: The iPod Home menu is dark again; V5.3I drew it as a light grey card. The PEQ editor and the Coverflow track list are dark again too. A build check now fails if any screen gains a light background or dark text that stock does not have.
- **V5.3R / V5.3I**: The compact release becomes the **iPod** release (`-ipod.zip`, About ends in `I`). It keeps compact's rows, hold-Return to Now Playing and pull to search, and restyles browsing after an iPod classic: flat full-width rows with a gradient selection bar, a status bar with the page title centred, a Home list menu with the current cover (Split or Full), right-hand chevrons, a large letter while spinning through long lists, and an iPod-style Now Playing with "3 of 12", the album, remaining time and Centre to scrub through the track. **Display settings** gains **Accent** (Graphite, Crimson, Tidal, Champagne), which replaces Shanling red everywhere, and **Home** (Split, Full). Confirm dialogs work with the wheel, and with Memory playback on the Q2 starts on Home with the last track paused. Both variants fix choppy AAC audio when a headset such as AirPods connects to the Q2 by itself.
- **V5.2R / V5.2C**: **PEQ: ON/OFF** now stays as you left it after a restart. Editing a band sets the preamp to just enough cut to keep boosts from clipping. Fast wheel spins on Coverflow no longer keep scrolling after the wheel stops or come to rest between two albums. The unused stock EQ preset images are removed, freeing rootfs space.
- **V5.1R / V5.1C**: Coverflow no longer keeps scrolling after you lift your finger: a swipe now settles on the album nearest to where you let go, instead of being thrown one or two albums further.
- **V5.0R / V5.0C**: Coverflow covers now reliably snap to the nearest album whenever they come to rest between two, including swipes the previous fix missed.
- **V4.9R / V4.9C**: Coverflow covers always snap into place when you let go of a swipe, instead of sometimes stopping between two albums.
- **V4.8R / V4.8C**: Coverflow tracks accept the Play/Pause hold queue menu (Play next / Add to queue), Coverflow remembers the last album and each album's highlighted track, and long album, artist and track names scroll. The payload is now built with `-Oz` so compact keeps room in the rootfs.
- **V4.7R / V4.7C**: Coverflow caches artwork in `.coverflow` on the microSD card and checks free space there, so low internal storage no longer prevents artwork preparation.
- **V4.6R / V4.6C**: New **Coverflow** card on Home, after Local Music: flip through your Local Music albums by cover with the wheel, open one to see its tracks, and play from there. The first open prepares the artwork once (from `cover.jpg`, `folder.jpg` or the embedded picture) with progress and Cancel; later opens only add new albums, and the last card, **Refresh library**, rebuilds it. The queue menu no longer shows a message after a successful add; it still shows **Queue unchanged** when it can't add.
- **V4.5R / V4.5C**: Hold **Play/Pause** on a highlighted song, album or folder in the local lists to open **Play next** / **Add to queue**. Tracks are added without pausing, restarting or seeking what is playing; a paused player stays paused, and with shuffle on, Play next is still the next track. Return closes the menu and the release after the hold never toggles playback.
- **V4.4R / V4.4C**: After a restart the PEQ editor now shows **PEQ: OFF**, matching the status-bar EQ icon and what you hear; the PEQ stays off after a reboot until you switch it back on, and your applied bands are kept.
- **V4.3R / V4.3C**: Saved PEQ presets can be deleted from **Presets → Delete a preset**, after a confirmation; the active EQ is unchanged.
- **V4.2R / V4.2C**: PEQ editor messages (Applied, Loaded, errors) now appear in the title bar instead of cutting the list down to two rows while they show. The title names the preset you loaded ("PEQ: HD650") and keeps the applied preset's name when you reopen the editor, until the next reboot.
- **V4.1R / V4.1C**: Artist pages now show their albums straight away; V3.8 highlighted the Album tab but opened an empty page until you switched tabs. PEQ editor lists no longer show a white strip below the last row.
- **V4.0R / V4.0C**: PEQ audio and import fixes. Band and preamp changes no longer click: filters keep their state across updates and the preamp is applied after them as a smooth ramp. Near-silent audio no longer drives the filters into slow denormal math. On low sample-rate files, a band above the file's frequency range is skipped instead of switching the whole EQ off. Imports match Equalizer APO more closely: LS/HS with a Q use APO's corner frequency, shelves without a Q use APO's default slope, comma decimals such as `-3,5` are accepted, empty `None` filter slots are skipped, and a shelf whose APO corner shift leaves 20-20000 Hz is clamped to the range instead of failing the import.
- **V3.9R / V3.9C**: The PEQ editor rows use white text instead of grey. The preamp is shown on one read-only row; set it in the preset (`Preamp:` in AutoEQ / Equalizer APO files) instead of the removed lower/raise controls. The editor drops its Back rows (Return steps back), shows only whole rows, shows the status line only when there is a message, and reads "Nothing to apply" until there are edits or a loaded preset to apply.
- **V3.8R / V3.8C**: Artist pages open on Albums; All Songs stays one tap away. The artist page tabs are now translated: stock firmware showed Chinese 单曲/专辑 there in every language.
- **V3.7R / V3.7C**: The stock equalizer is replaced by a ten-band parametric EQ with peaking and shelf bands, preamp, an immediate on/off switch that also drives the status-bar EQ icon, saved presets and AutoEQ / Equalizer APO `.txt` import from `/EQ` on the microSD card. Return steps back through the editor's screens. At the ends of the local folder and music lists the wheel now hard-stops while you keep turning; pause briefly, then turn again to wrap around.
- **V3.6R / V3.6C**: Compact Local Songs opens search with a pull-down. Start inside the list while it is at the top; the overlay reads "Pull to search" after an 8-pixel downward drag and "Release to search" at 48 pixels. Releasing opens the stock search dialog, and moving back below the threshold cancels. The gesture clears on wheel or button input, navigation, screen-off or interruption and never activates a row. Empty lists work; Folder view and other lists are unchanged, and the normal build keeps its stock search toolbar.
- **V3.5R / V3.5C**: In compact mode, ordinary list-row titles use the full row width on every native relayout, reserving space only for visible artwork and trailing controls. Widths follow scrolling, row reuse and artwork/control visibility changes; short titles stay at their left position and overflowing titles still scroll or ellipsize. Other layouts and the normal build are unchanged.
- **V3.4R / V3.4C**: The local folder and music lists carry over at their ends: the first turn past an end nudges the selection outline against the end, and the next turn in the same direction wraps to the other end of the same list. Wheel navigation now wakes the player's own scrollbar, which fades on its normal timer, instead of drawing a separate position bar. The local and online search result lists navigate with the wheel and the centre button; the on-screen keyboard dialogs keep the wheel on volume control. Compact Return now opens Now Playing on hold and stays there after release; the next short Return goes back to where you were, and holding Return while already on Now Playing uses the stock Home shortcut. This replaces the V3.3 single-press latch behavior.
- **V3.3R / V3.3C**: Compact returns from Now Playing with a single Return press again; the stock hold-release latch is cleared on that page instead of swallowing the release and forcing a second press.
- **V3.2R / V3.2C**: Compact rows now fill the client area with 72-pixel rows, so the stock artwork keeps its natural size with an even eight-pixel inset instead of being scaled down. Navigation and all other behavior are unchanged.
- **V3.1R / V3.1C**: Shared normal and compact builds, compact local lists and long Return to Now Playing, and a reproducible dual-variant release procedure.
- **V3.0R**: Long lists accelerate smoothly again: each 100 ms of sustained same-direction spin adds a row to the step, up to eight rows per tick, replacing the V2.7R/V2.8R three-speed ladder. Stopping, reversing or easing off still drops back to one row immediately. The selected-row outline is now a softer translucent white line, so it sits better against the dark theme while the dark separator still keeps it readable over bright album art. The shipped `config.ini` is no longer modified, so a fresh install keeps the stock key tone default; a device that already ran V2.9R keeps its saved setting and can change it in the system settings.
- **V2.9R**: Restores the stock wheel and button input path, removing the V2.8R 25 ms detent hold. The key tone now ships disabled, so wheel and button feedback is silent by default; enable **Key Tone** in the system settings to bring the clicks back. The three-speed wheel acceleration from V2.8R is unchanged.
- **V2.8R**: Wheel acceleration adds a third speed: longer lists step two rows after 300 ms and three rows after 600 ms of continuous same-direction turns. A wheel button press no longer plays a second tick from the capacitive touch, drops the phantom step with it, and the wheel is ready again as soon as the button is released.
- **V2.7R**: Wheel turns move the list immediately instead of animating, and lists longer than 16 rows step two rows after 450 ms of continuous same-direction turns. Touching the screen hides the selection outline until the next accepted wheel or centre action, including across page changes. Centre confirmation and the double-press screen toggle now use a 200 ms window.
- **V2.6R**: Wheel input replaces an unfinished scroll-view animation so reversing a glide toward a list boundary takes effect immediately. Native clicks also reset wheel acceleration and the home wheel interval when no touch-down event was delivered.
- **V2.5R**: Native clicks cancel pending centre confirmation even without a touch-down event. Rejected confirmations leave changed lists and their remembered positions untouched. Double-press still turns the screen off after interrupting a recalled list with touch. Custom boot logos must use the stock renderer's supported JPEG frame format; packaging uses a snapshot of the validated image.

- **V2.3R**: Wheel selection and restored positions keep a small margin from the screen edge, and reversing the home wheel responds immediately to correct an overshoot.
